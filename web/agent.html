Run the agent in your visa tab

No extension, no app. A bookmark runs the agent inside your logged-in visa account, checks every consulate for you, alerts you, and shares the dates here for everyone.

On a computer

  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar.
    Visa agent
    No bookmarks bar? Press Ctrl+Shift+B (Cmd+Shift+B on Mac).
  2. Log in to your visa account (ais.usvisa-info.com or usvisascheduling.com) and open the appointment or reschedule page, where you choose a consulate.
  3. Click the bookmark. A small panel opens in the corner. Choose your visa type and press Start.
  4. Keep that tab open. You can use other tabs. If you reload or get signed out, log in again and click the bookmark again.

On a phone

  1. Copied
  2. Bookmark any page, then edit that bookmark: name it Visa agent and paste the copied text as its address.
  3. On your visa appointment page, run it. Chrome: type “Visa agent” in the address bar and tap the bookmark. Safari: open Bookmarks and tap it.

Phones pause pages in the background, so checks only run while the visa tab is on screen. A computer works better for leaving it running.

Good to know

  • It never logs in or solves a CAPTCHA. You log in yourself; it works inside that session.
  • It does what you would do by hand: picks each consulate in the dropdown, a few seconds apart, every 10 minutes by default (5 at least).
  • You get a sound, a browser notification and a flashing tab title when an earlier date opens. Turn on alerts on the Alerts tab to also get them on your phone.
  • Its dates show here as Verified, because they come straight from the booking site.
  • Your settings stay in your browser. Nothing about your account is sent, only consulate names and open dates.

Which visa sites it works on

  • ais.usvisa-info.com (Canada, Mexico, Brazil, Colombia, Europe and others): works.
  • usvisascheduling.com (India, China, Nigeria and others): this site has strict security rules that can stop bookmarks. If clicking the bookmark does nothing, or the panel says uploads are blocked, use the Chrome extension there instead.
